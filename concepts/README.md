CREATOR GUIDANCE

Describe the concept of modularizing components, perhaps with a wireframed example from either a real website or a demo site. Re-iterate the whole state-re-render cycle making it important that components are isolated and able to update and re-render themselves independent of the whole app. A major benefit of react is being able to just re-render the parts that change and keep the rest the same.

Communicate that there is no "one" answer to how to organize components in a react app, and one of the most common problems with react is changing your mind mid-app on how your components should be structured. Don't fear the refactor, but do diagram your components and have a plan for them! Re-assure students that planning out components takes practice and experience.
